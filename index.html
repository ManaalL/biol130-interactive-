About the course

BIOL 130 at the University of Waterloo introduces the concepts of cell biology. It focuses on two things: how the cell is structurally organized, and how the key molecular processes that define living things work.

Course description paraphrased from the University of Waterloo Undergraduate Calendar. The course textbook is Morris et al., Biology: How Life Works, 4th edition (Macmillan Learning, 2023).

How to use these

Work through a module's chapters in order, or jump to one topic. Every chapter points back to pages in your course notes.
