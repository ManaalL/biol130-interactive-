How each module works

Four modes on every module page, plus a review loop that runs across all of them.

01 · Before classRead and PrepRead is the notes, cleaned up, with the blanks filled in and the figures alongside. Prep takes 5 minutes: you guess at three questions first, then get the big picture and the words you'll hear.
02 · LearnChapters you doShort sections that follow the course notes with page numbers. Each one has figures, simulations and questions, and your first-try score is saved.
03 · After classCheckWrite down everything you remember, compare it with the notes, then take a short quiz. Anything you miss or guess goes into your daily review.
04 · Every dayDaily reviewSpaced, mixed review across every module you've covered: questions, terms, diagrams with the labels covered, and explain-it prompts.
