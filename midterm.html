Midterm prep: Modules 1 to 5

Three ways in. Take a practice midterm and get a breakdown by chapter. Work through every review question your prof gave, check your answer, and rate how well you knew it. Then follow the study plan your results build.

Review questions are the BIOL 130 Modules 1 to 5 review list. Answers for Modules 1 to 4 follow your course notes, with page numbers. Module 5 answers follow the textbook until the Module 5 notes are added. An independent study aid, not an official University of Waterloo resource.
